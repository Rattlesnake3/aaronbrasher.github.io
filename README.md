Overview
This repository hosts my GitHub Pages engineering portfolio, showcasing the work I completed during my senior year in the Mechatronics Engineering program at Washington State University. It highlights my interdisciplinary experience across aerospace, embedded systems, automation, electrical engineering, and systems integration.

The centerpiece of this portfolio is my senior capstone project: the design and integration of a Fish Containment and Well‑Being Management System within a larger Aquaponics Process Industry System, including SCADA‑based monitoring and supervisory control.

Senior Capstone — Aquaponics Process Industry System
Fish Containment & Well‑Being Management Subsystem
This subsystem was developed to ensure the safe, humane, and efficient operation of the aquaponics system located in the main mechatronics lab. The system integrates:

Embedded hardware (ESP32, microcontrollers, sensor interfaces)

Real‑time data acquisition for water quality and environmental monitoring

SCADA integration for supervisory control, alarms, and operator visibility

Mechanical containment design for fish safety and flow management

Systems engineering documentation, including requirements, diagrams, and validation testing

The goal was to create a reliable, maintainable subsystem that supports fish health while contributing to the overall aquaponics process loop.
